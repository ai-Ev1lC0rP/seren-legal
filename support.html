How can we help?

Quick answers for listening, Premium, purchases, and your private data.

Restore Premium

Open Profile in La-Lo-Ka, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Manage a subscription

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then choose Subscriptions and La-Lo-Ka.

A sound will not play

Open the Sounds tab and start the sound again. Check your device volume and disconnect any Bluetooth device you are not using.

Background listening

Start a sound, then lock your iPhone or switch apps. Playback controls remain available from the Lock Screen and Control Center.

Your mood history

Mood check-ins stay on your iPhone. Deleting La-Lo-Ka also removes those entries; they are not stored on our servers.

Request support

Describe what happened, your iPhone model, and the iOS version. Never include passwords or payment details.
