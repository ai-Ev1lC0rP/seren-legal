Seren Support

Seren is a local-first sleep & focus soundscape mixer for iPhone.

Help

  • Subscriptions: Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Restore purchases: Profile → Restore Purchases (or Paywall → Restore).
  • Sounds not playing: Check Silent Mode / Focus, then restart playback from the Sounds tab.
