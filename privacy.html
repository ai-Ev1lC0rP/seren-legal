Seren Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Seren (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a local-first relaxation and soundscape app. There is no account, no sign-in, and most data never leaves your device.

1. What we collect — and where it lives

1.1 Mood notes (on-device only)

When you log a mood check-in, that entry is stored in Apple UserDefaults on your device. It is not transmitted to any server. If you delete the app, this data is removed.

1.2 Listening session state (on-device only)

Playback state, selected tracks, saved soundscape presets, and free-tier listening timers are stored in UserDefaults on your device. None of this is uploaded or shared.

1.3 Subscription status (Apple)

Premium subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit 2. Seren receives only a boolean entitlement signal from Apple (premium: yes/no) — not your payment method, billing address, or purchase history. Apple’s privacy practices are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

1.4 Free-tier listening breaks

Free users see a short in-app listening break overlay at intervals. No ad SDK is currently integrated. The overlay is a first-party Seren screen with no third-party tracking, network calls, or data collection.

If we integrate a third-party ad network in a future version, this policy will be updated before release to disclose what the SDK collects.

2. What we do not collect

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No names, emails, phone numbers, or contact information collected by Seren
  • No location tracking
  • No analytics SDKs (Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.) at this time
  • No sharing of your data with third parties for marketing

3. Apple platform data

Seren runs on iOS. Apple may collect usage and crash data if you have opted into Analytics & Improvements or App Analytics in iOS Settings. That is governed by Apple.

4. Children

Seren is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention

All on-device data persists until you delete the app or clear it. We hold no server-side data to delete.

6. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no server-side personal data to access, export, or delete. Your on-device data is under your control by deleting the app. For Apple purchase data, contact Apple.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when we add features. The updated date at the top will change. We will not reduce privacy protections without an in-app notice for material changes.

8. Contact

Questions: open an issue on our support page or email via the contact listed on Support.
