Best rain sounds apps for sleep in 2026

By Cason Clark, maker of La-Lo-Ka. Disclosure: La-Lo-Ka is our app. Details on other apps come from their App Store pages as of mid-October 2026; prices and features change, so check before you buy.

"Rain sounds app" means three different things: a rain mixer (stack layers, set a timer), a noise machine (white, pink or brown noise to mask a noisy street), and a full sleep app (stories, meditation, tracking). Pick the job first, then the app.

Quick picks

  • Free, nothing to install: Apple's built-in Background Sounds.
  • Biggest free rain catalog: Rain Rain Sleep Sounds.
  • Classic noise machine: White Noise Lite.
  • Tweak every frequency: myNoise.
  • Sleep stories and meditation: Calm or BetterSleep.
  • Rain-first mixing with Night Mode and no account: La-Lo-Ka (ours).

1. Apple Background Sounds (built in, free)

Settings → Accessibility → Audio & Visual → Background Sounds. It plays rain, ocean, stream and noise options and can keep playing when other audio starts. No mixing and limited choice, but it costs nothing and needs no download. Try it first if one rain track is all you need.

2. Rain Rain Sleep Sounds

A long-running favorite. Its App Store listing says the free tier includes 50+ sounds, a mixer with per-sound volume, fade-out timers, Oscillator and Soften, with a small banner ad; Premium adds more sounds and removes the ad. Best for: people who want lots of rain variety free.

3. White Noise Lite

A classic noise machine with white, pink, brown and other noise colors plus nature sounds, free with ads. Best for: masking traffic or a snoring partner more than "rain mood".

4. myNoise

Sound generators with sliders for each frequency band. Best for: tinkerers who want to shape noise precisely.

5. Calm and BetterSleep

Full sleep platforms with stories, meditations and large sound libraries behind a subscription. Best for: people who want guided content, not just rain.

6. La-Lo-Ka (our app)

We built La-Lo-Ka because we wanted rain first and nothing else in the way. Four rain families (window, tent, metal, porch), thunder, fire, ocean, fan and noise colors; a sleep timer with a soft fade; and Night Mode, which restarts last night's mix and timer with one tap from the widget, Control Center or Siri. No account, no ads, App Store privacy label "Data Not Collected".

The honest trade-off: free is 10 sounds, one at a time (plus a Premium sound to try each week). Mixing layers, Mix Lab and saved presets are Premium (first 7 nights free, then $29.99/year or $4.99/month). If you only ever want one sound, Background Sounds or Rain Rain's free tier may be all you need.

How to choose in 30 seconds

  1. Need to mask noise? Pick a noise machine or add brown noise under rain.
  2. Want rain that feels like a place? Pick a mixer and layer it.
  3. Want someone to talk you to sleep? Pick a full sleep app.
  4. Always use a timer with a fade so the sound doesn't stop abruptly or run all night.
