Why mix rain instead of looping one track?

One rain recording gets old, and a loud one can wake you up. Layering lets you set the rain where you want it: steady tent rain as the bed, thunder pushed far back, a quiet fire for warmth. Each layer has its own volume, so nothing spikes.

What's in the app

  • Four rain families: window, tent, metal and porch rain.
  • Storms and more: thunderstorm, rolling and crackling thunder, ocean waves, crackling and quiet fire, a box fan, white, pink and brown noise.
  • Sleep timer with a soft fade, plus a Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
  • Night Mode: one tap starts last night's mix and timer, from Home, the widget, Control Center or Siri.
  • Mix Lab (Premium): Oscillator, Soften and pan to shape each layer.
  • Private by design: App Store label "Data Not Collected". Mixes and mood notes stay on your iPhone.
